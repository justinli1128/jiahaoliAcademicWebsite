Research Interests

I am mainly interested in Homotopy Theoretic topics, such as Functor Calculus, Chromatic Homotopy Theory, Motivic Homotopy Theory, etc.

I am also interested in other topics such as Mathematical Quantum Physics, Mathematical Biology, Stochastic Processes, etc.

Papers

On the Classifying Space of Homogeneous Functors

This is my research paper on the classifying space of homogeneous functors in manifold calculus. The main result of this paper shows that the classifying space of homogeneous functors over any manifolds is weakly equivalent to the classifying space of a simplicial monoid.

Presentations

Geometric Quantization in Mathematical Quantum Physics

This is the slide show for my presentation at the Mathematical Biology Research Group at UBCO on Geometric Quantization. It is the closing presentation for my directed study on Mathematical Quantum Physics under the supervision of Dr. Foxall at UBCO. It covers Hamiltonian mechanics on symplectic manifolds, basics in Quantum mechanics, and Geometric quantization, with each steps in the construction, prequantum line bundle, prequantization, polarization, half-form correction, and BKS construction explained.

My Notes

Algebraic Geometry in Chromatic Homotopy Theory This is my notes when I studied Chromatic Homotopy Theory, focused on how it relates to Algebraic Geometry. It is not very well written, but I think it has most of the stuffs one should know in CHT. Mostly discussion on the connection between geometry and homotopy, no proofs (I think). It talks about complex oriented cohomology, formal groups and laws, moduli stack of formal groups (MU and MP, Landweber Exact,...), height filtration (BP, E(n),...), Chromatic Convergence, Lubin-Tate stuffs.
